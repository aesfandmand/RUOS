# Owner Decisions — Work Export Continuation

Date: 2026-08-06
Status: preserved source material

This file preserves the decisions supplied directly by the owner after the Work conversation export.

## Architecture decisions

- A comprehensive investment hub is required.
- Indoor structure purchase requires a dedicated page for the defined business personas.
- Outdoor structure purchase requires a dedicated page for municipalities, organizations and other entities capable of installation.
- Billboard rental and campaign execution is an independent advertising journey.
- The Structures page must be a complete catalog and knowledge reference, not a single-purpose conversion page.
- A structure record may route to purchase, investment, rental or organizational pages where relevant.
- Blog and video content must be present across important pages where contextually relevant.

## Structures data ambition

The Structures page should make it enjoyable and easy for a user to find any relevant information about a structure, including:

- categories and classifications;
- photographs and models;
- foundations and drawings;
- objectives and functions;
- suitable locations;
- dimensions;
- number of faces;
- horizontal or vertical orientation;
- front/back lighting models;
- technical, media, spatial, engineering and commercial attributes.

These structure entities must become reusable source data for product, rental, investment, institutional sales and every other site area that needs structure information.

## Knowledge graph and capability

- The knowledge-graph layer must appear on every page according to its pillar, cluster and topic family.
- The Capability layer must be defined early and explicitly: where it acts, why it acts and how it proves the organization's ability.
- Neither layer may be deferred until the end of page production.

## Process decision

The architecture must be reviewed again, component by component, with the owner. Any required edits must be made before page design begins. Each approved layer becomes the source of truth for the next step.
