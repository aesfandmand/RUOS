# Motion strategy references — sent 2026-10-13

Seven screen recordings. The owner's own framing of why they matter:

> مسئله من رنگ نیست — حرکت و استراتژی تولید صفحات است.
> میخوام متوجه بشی که دنبال چه چیزایی میگردم: مثلا تنوع و حرکت چگونه باشه،
> و یا شکل موس در صفحات و بخش‌هایی می‌تواند تغییر کند و حتی روایت بسازد.

So this set is not a mood board. It is the source of the **verb inventory**
(`05-rules/website/motion-verbs-v1.yaml`) and of design model **§21**, which
governs how a page's motion is planned before its content is written.

`clips/` holds each recording at 640px, no audio. `stills/` holds the frames
worth looking at without opening the clip. **Watch the clip** before building
any verb — a still cannot show you what the section does.

| # | Clip | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | `xurya-white-b2b-scroll-score` | The whole argument in one page: white-dominant B2B, advanced without a dark stage. Brand intro, stat strip overlapping the hero photo, count-ups, feature grid filling cell by cell, testimonial tabs swapping one card, a single charcoal band at the close. ## What these do not license

- **Colour.** The owner said so directly: colour is not the question here.
  §1 is unchanged — white dominates, charcoal once, red at the close.
  References 03, 04, 05 and 07 are dark; take their verbs, not their ground.
- **Numbers.** Several of these count up figures we have no equivalent for.
  §3 still governs: an unverified figure gets a tagged placeholder and no
  count-up.
- **The navigation.** §1 of CLAUDE.md. Every one of these has a header; ours
  is locked and does not learn anything from them.
