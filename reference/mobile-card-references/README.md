## The pattern the three share

1. **Generous radii** — roughly 20–28px on cards, fully round on chips,
   pills and avatars.
2. **Photos bleed to the card's rounded edge.** No photo sits in a padded
   frame inside a card.
3. **Rows are asymmetric.** A row is two cells of *different* widths, or a
   text block beside an image block — never a uniform grid repeated down
   the page.
4. **Badges overlay the photo** (price, lesson number, a small round mark),
   they do not sit in the text area.
5. **Text stack under a photo is title → subtitle → meta**, each step
   smaller and lighter than the one above.
6. **Section headers pair a title with a link on the far side** ("Top
   trips" / "Explore →").
7. **The card carries its own action** — a pill button inside the card,
   not a bare link at the bottom of a list.
8. **Category rows scroll horizontally** as icon+label cells, and can be
   clipped at the edge to signal more.
